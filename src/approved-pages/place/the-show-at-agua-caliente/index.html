The Show at Agua Caliente

A favorite Rancho Mirage night out for tribute bands and headliners, with loge seats at the top of our list.

Live MusicTribute BandsHeadlinersDate Night
♡ Darcey's Favorite
Shows & TicketsThe SteakhouseDirections

♡ Darcey's Take

“We love this venue: great shows, amazing talent, and in our experience, not a bad seat in the house. We've been in floor seats, loge seats and even the last row in the upper deck. Every seat has been wonderful, but the loge is our favorite. The tribute bands are amazing, and so are the headliners. Don't miss dinner before the show or drinks afterward at The Steakhouse!”
