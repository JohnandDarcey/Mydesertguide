“When it comes to pest control, I always recommend Mr. Beez Termite & Pest Control. Yes, Barrett, the owner, is my nephew - but I would recommend them even if he was not family. Barrett has built an outstanding reputation throughout the Coachella Valley by doing things the right way: showing up on time, communicating clearly, treating customers with respect and standing behind every job. Whether you need regular pest service, termite inspections or help with one of those unexpected desert critters, Mr. Beez is thorough, professional and incredibly dependable. They are one of those companies I never hesitate to recommend because I know they will take great care of you - just like they have taken care of so many of my friends, clients and neighbors.”
