♡ Darcey's Take

“Escena is beautiful from start to finish. The course has big mountain views, and the clubhouse has that mid-century modern Palm Springs look everyone loves. Even if you are not playing, it is worth going for lunch or drinks because the setting is so good. This is a great pick when you want golf to feel stylish and very Palm Springs.”
