♡ Darcey's Take

“World Market is one of my favorite places in Palm Springs for unique home decor, furniture, gourmet foods, gifts and entertaining essentials from around the world, all at surprisingly approachable prices. Whether you are decorating a new home, refreshing your patio or picking up a thoughtful hostess gift, there is always something interesting to discover. I especially love visiting during the holidays, when the seasonal displays are full of one-of-a-kind finds.”
