Palm Springs · Mountain Experience

Palm Springs Aerial Tramway

One of the most iconic Palm Springs experiences, with dramatic views, cooler mountain air, hiking, dining and that unforgettable ride up the canyon.

Mountain ExperienceBig views
♡ Darcey's Favorite
WebsiteDirections

♡ Darcey's Take

“The Tram is one of those experiences everyone should do at least once. You leave the desert floor and suddenly you are up in the mountains with completely different views and cooler air. Bring a light layer, even if it is warm in town, because the top can feel like a totally different season. Give yourself time to walk around, take photos, and enjoy the view before heading back down.”
