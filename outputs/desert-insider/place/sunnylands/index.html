Rancho Mirage · Gardens & Culture

Sunnylands

A beautifully designed Rancho Mirage landmark with modern architecture, desert gardens and a calm, polished setting that feels special without feeling fussy.

Gardens & CultureArchitecturegardensa peaceful desert outing

♡ Darcey's Take

“Sunnylands is peaceful, beautiful, and such a nice change of pace. Check the tour schedule before you go if you want to see more of the estate, but even just walking the gardens is worth it. Leave time to wander, enjoy the architecture, and take in the mountain views. It is one of those places that feels calm, polished, and very special.”
