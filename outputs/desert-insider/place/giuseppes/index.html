♡ Darcey's Take

“Giuseppe's is such a great neighborhood Italian spot, especially when you want something casual, comfortable, and dependable. In season, get there early if you want a bar seat because it fills up fast. The happy hour pizza is a great value, and I love pairing it with the Caesar salad. It is one of those places where you can keep it simple and still leave happy.”
