♡ Darcey's Take

“Cimarron is one of my favorite value plays in the desert. The course is usually in good condition, the pricing is strong, and it is an easy recommendation when someone wants a fun round without overcomplicating it. Stay after your round if you can - the restaurant and sunset views are worth it.”
