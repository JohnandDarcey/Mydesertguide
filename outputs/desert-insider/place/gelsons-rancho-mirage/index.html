♡ Darcey's Take

“Gelson's Rancho Mirage has a wine bar inside the store, which makes shopping feel a little more fun. You can sip while you shop, watch fresh sushi being made, grab a Wolfgang Puck pizza, or stop by the carvery for prime rib or turkey. The deli has some of the most interesting salads, too. I love the idea of doing your shopping and then staying for lunch at the wine bar.”
