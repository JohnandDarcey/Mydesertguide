♡ Darcey's Take

“Bongo Johnny's is a fun, casual brunch spot when you want something easy in the middle of Palm Springs. It is relaxed, dog-friendly, and great for a low-pressure breakfast or lunch with friends. Go with a laid-back mindset, grab a patio seat if you can, and enjoy the people-watching.”
